## Project 1: South Africa Crime Statistics (2013–2023)

## Key Findings
- **Gauteng** has the highest total crime volume (~16.2 million incidents), driven largely by theft and robbery.
- **Kidnapping** surged by **79%** in 2021–2022 and then another **42%** in 2022–2023 — making it the fastest-growing serious crime.
- **COVID-19 lockdown (2020–2021)** caused a sharp drop in movement-dependent crimes (burglary, drug-related crime), while interpersonal crimes (murder, rape) stayed relatively stable.
- **KwaZulu-Natal** leads in **murder**, overtaking Gauteng since 2021.
- **Western Cape** shows disproportionately high **drug-related crime** relative to its population, reflecting gang activity.
- **Commercial crime** exceeds **100,000 incidents** per year — the largest crime category by volume.
- Increased reports of **sexual offences detected as police action** may reflect improved policing/reporting rather than an actual increase in incidents.
